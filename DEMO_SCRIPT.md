# PACT — 90-Second Demo Script

## Setup
- Open localhost:3001 in browser
- Demo Controls panel visible on right
- Start at Welcome screen

---

## Script (90 seconds)

### Opening (10s)
**[Welcome Screen]**
"This is Pact. We help friends save money together by making a commitment and holding each other accountable."

→ Click **Get Started**

### The Problem (5s)
**[Onboarding Step 1]**
"People intend to save, but their goals fade when everyday spending decisions arise. Budgets explain yesterday — Pact changes the next decision."

→ Click **Home** in demo panel

### Home (10s)
**[Home Screen]**
"Radhika has saved $370 toward her Italy trip through past Pacts. Her Circle is Cornell Crew — herself, Ketan, and Yuge."

→ Click **Create Pact** in demo panel

### Create a Pact (15s)
**[Challenge Selection]**
"She picks Delivery Detox — no food delivery apps Monday through Thursday. 7 days, 4 eligible days."

→ Click Delivery Detox → Quickly tap through Duration → Circle → Consequence (Karaoke Confession) → Savings ($10/day toward Italy)

→ Click **Active Pact** in demo panel

### Active Pact (10s)
**[Active Dashboard]**
"Day 4 of 4. Radhika is 3 for 3 with a $30 allocation toward Italy. Ketan missed a day. Everyone can see the standings."

→ Click **App Temptation** in demo panel

### The Key Moment (10s)
**[DoorDash Interception]**
"Wednesday, 6:45 PM. She opens DoorDash. Pact intervenes: 'You're 3 of 4 days in. Cornell Crew is doing this with you. Remember Italy?' She keeps her pact."

→ Click **Everyone Succeeds** in demo panel

### Results — Everyone Wins (10s)
**[Celebration Screen]**
"Everyone crushed it — 4 for 4. Group celebration, no consequence needed. Radhika saved $40 this week, now at $380 toward Italy."

→ Click **Unique Last Place** in demo panel

### Results — Someone Loses (10s)
**[Consequence Screen]**
"But what if Ketan finished last? He owes the group a Karaoke Confession — a fun, pre-agreed dare. No money changes hands. He submits video evidence, the group votes to verify."

→ Click **Weekly Recap** in demo panel

### Retention Loop (10s)
**[Weekly Recap]**
"Weekly recap shows progress. One-tap renewal keeps the momentum going. The loop: commit, participate, celebrate, repeat."

→ Click **Renew Pact** in demo panel

### Close (10s)
**[Renew Screen]**
"Saving becomes easier to stick with when friends make the commitment visible, enjoyable, and repeatable. That's Pact."

---

## Backup Demo States

If you need to skip to a specific moment:
- **All Tied Below**: Shows the group reset scenario (no punishment)
- **Two Tied Last**: Shows shared consequences
- **Consequence Unresolved**: Shows the gentle escalation path
- **Savings Progress**: Shows cumulative savings and simulated accounts

## Tips
- Use demo panel to jump between states; don't try to click through every step
- The app temptation screen is the emotional climax — pause there
- Emphasize: no financial penalties, no real money movement, playful accountability
