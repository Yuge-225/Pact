Value Proposition

What PACT adds over a group chat

Group Chat + Willpower

  • ❌ No tracking or structure
  • ❌ No streak to protect
  • ❌ No stakes or consequence
  • ❌ No savings connection
  • ❌ Fizzles in 3 days

PACT

  • ✅ Structured challenges with clear rules
  • 🔥 Streaks, XP, daily gamification
  • 🎤 Playful dares — fun, not punitive
  • 💰 Each day → $ toward your goal
  • 🔄 7-day cycles, renew, repeat

Feasibility

Can we make it?

⚙️ Technical

  • ✓ Core loop built — 15 screens, 20 states
  • ✓ Standard mobile engineering
  • △ Bank linking via Plaid — established API
  • ✗ iOS app interception — not feasible

🏢 Operational

  • ✓ Onboarding — 3-minute flow
  • ✓ Consequence moderation — scalable
  • △ Circle activation — 3+ friends must download
  • △ Dispute resolution — not yet designed

💰 Financial

  • ✓ Freemium — $5.99/mo premium
  • △ Break-even at ~2K MAU, 7% conversion
  • △ Plaid costs ~$0.30/user/month

Central Business Logic
People will buy PACT because

Saving alone is boring and easy to quit — a structured challenge with friends, daily streaks, and playful social consequences creates the accountability and fun that turns good intentions into actual behavior change.

We can make PACT because

The core social loop is technically straightforward, banking APIs exist for account linking, and the gamification patterns that drive daily engagement are well-proven by Duolingo and Strava.
